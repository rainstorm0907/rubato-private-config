## Conclusion

- Main browse gets its value from photos piled in a familiar area, and from discovering photos in an unexpected area and zooming there. A single place is not chosen first. Visit-linking is not a required motive outside Recap.
- Correction is not the protagonist. Do not put a large merge action on the main browse. Place correction (name, merge, split) is outside 1.0; do not invent its entry from this note. Succession, if that screen opens, is in [[decisions/maplog-place-visit-correction-lineage.md]] and `/Users/wooojin/App/maplog/record/PRODUCT.md`.
- The gallery is the sheet in `code/DESIGN.md` and the map-gallery section of PRODUCT. The 2026-09-02 album panel, and its 40% height cap, are not that sheet.
- Briefs use Korean that a developer, a planner, and a user can take the same way. Do not center code identifiers, and do not force a translation that creates a second meaning for the same word.

## Rationale

- Chose area-and-group looking around after the user separated that satisfaction from linking visits. Linking, if it is the product's job, is Recap.
- Rejected: assuming the user opens one place in order to continue a visit, and forcing a create-a-journey flow because a place or a photo was opened.
- Rejected: a wide cluster that hides the photos someone wants to open. “지금 보고싶은게 그냥 다 합쳐져버려” objects to that, and does not mean never tidy the screen.
- Rejected: treating the 2026-09-02 “40%까진 줄여줘” as the current gallery height, and treating that screen as still awaiting approval. The gallery sheet is the current browse.
