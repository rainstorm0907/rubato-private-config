## User correction

- 2026-09-04, in the context of investigating how Maple patches, Woojin: “A. 버프 프리저·펜던트 슬롯 일단 내가 생각한거랑은 좀 달라. 이렇게 직접적인 대체 + 보완이 아니야. B도. 오히려 C 가 더 가까워.”
- Status: user confirmed.
- Meaning: The core pattern is not directly scrapping and replacing an inconvenient structure. It is closer to spotting a desire users were already spending money to solve through unofficial trades, proxies, and external markets, then folding it into the game via Maple Points, the official exchange, and limited paid services so it creates a positive effect, even if the unofficial path remains.
- Strong reference point: Meso Market.
- Excluded or classified separately: direct replacement and supplementation cases such as making Buff Freezer and pendant slots the default, and Monster Life → Union Artifact.

## 2026-09-04 additional investigation

- Status: user confirmed.
- 2026-09-04, Woojin: “ㅇㅇ 정확해 너가 말한것들 이제 나도 납득이 된다.”
- Canonical case: Meso Market. While cracking down on cash trades, it partially absorbs cash↔meso demand into a safe, traceable Maple Point↔meso market. Unofficial trades remain because they are cheaper.
- Strong extension case: hunting, daily, and weekly labor that power-leveling shops sold is limited and priced as symbol/epic-dungeon quick passes, MVP Red·Black free quick-pass bundles, MVP Resort, VIP Sauna, and the like. Crackdowns and official products run in parallel.
- Likely case: Genesis Pass officially sells, among the products the liberation-proxy market sold, calendar time and party access as 3× traces, 6-person liberation parties, and stat buffs. Control proxies remain or combine with the pass, so it is partial absorption, not complete replacement.
- Intermediate case: New Name Auction trades rare nicknames by Maple Point auction, and right after launch the registration cap rose from 20,000→40,000. Evidence connecting it to the existing unofficial nickname market is relatively weak.
- Intermediate case: the world-integrated Maple Auction opens cross-world trades of equipment and cash items, and takes 10% of the price in Maple Points for purchases from other worlds. Official liquidity expansion is clear, but the causal claim that it aims to absorb external trades is weak.
- Excluded: Genesis Pass PLUS, ordinary presets and schedulers, making Buff Freezer the default, Monster Life replacement, and cube meso-ization heavily affected by regulation.
