## Hiring-platform notification preferences

- Keep recommended-posting notifications, company and recruiter contact, and application and interview status notifications, so a hiring opportunity is not missed.
- Does not want education, conference, discount, content, event, game, or general marketing notifications.
- If the same recommendation is duplicated as email and text, keep only the email and turn the text off.

On 2026-08-28, on Wanted, only the recommended-position email was kept, and the recommended-position text plus the benefits and event email and text were turned off. On LinkedIn, job-posting recommendation, alert, and application-status emails were kept, and the other email notifications, including general-message and InMail emails, were turned off.
